# Uproot Clean: the original ad and its two iterations

From Spencer's keynote (14:00–27:35). Uproot Clean sells an enzyme tablet for washing machines and
is the brand our Maskinrent ads are based on. He labels the original a breakthrough with "99.9 %"
confidence (found via an ad spy tool sorted by shares). Both iterations also became breakthroughs, and
iteration 2 is their top ad in the Ad Library.

## The original (grandpa, solution aware)
Lines shown or heard in the talk:
- On-screen hook: "My grandpa used to fix washing machines for a living. This is what he told me to do
  about the smell."
- "…underwear. Nothing smelled clean anymore. At first, I blamed the detergent. Switched brands twice.
  Then I blamed the dog, because honestly, what else do you blame when your house smells like damp
  [fur]?"
- "I tried extra rinse cycles. I tried running hot water with vinegar. I tried wiping down the rubber
  gasket with bleach. I even bought [a machine cleaner] from Target and ran it once a month like the
  box said. Nothing worked."
- "He walked into the laundry room, opened the door, leaned in, and took one sniff. Then he looked at
  me and said, 'You're not washing your clothes. You're slow cooking them in mold.'"
- "Only way to truly clean the source of what is happening inside a washing machine is with enzymes.
  And this tablet is the only one he has found that has all the necessary ones to truly destroy the
  buildup. He said the tablet has three specific enzymes that do three different jobs."
- Then (described, not played): alpha amylase dissolves detergent and film, protease breaks down the
  protein in pet hair, lipase strips the greasy layer from oils; "first and only enzyme-based
  cleaning tablet".

**On screen:** fast B-roll, about one shot per spoken line (~2 s), with a small white caption mid-screen.
The hook text sits on a close-up of the drum. Then a woman sniffing a blanket, detergent being poured, a
golden retriever by the washer and a man on a sofa sniffing. Clip 2 shows the door gasket being scrubbed
and a machine-cleaner pack. The grandpa is on camera: an older man in a cap in a yellow laundry room
leaning in to sniff. The mechanism part shows the tablet over the drum dissolving in a cyan foam graphic,
then a clean drum.

| Component | In the original |
|---|---|
| Angle | It will fix your bad-smelling laundry |
| Avatar | Pet owner whose machine doesn't smell good. Experiences: clothes musty for months, house smells like damp fur, tried a known cleaner. Behaviors: switched detergents, bleach on the gasket, extra rinses. Emotions: frustrated, grossed out. |
| Desire | Health ("I want a clean machine" → a health hazard) |
| Mechanism | Three enzymes, each tied to a benefit |
| Authority | Grandpa who repaired washing machines: in the hook, "one sniff", recommends the mechanism |
| Awareness | Solution aware (lists failed solutions, doesn't agitate) |
| Emotion | Unpleasant, low intensity |
| Positioning | First and only enzyme-based cleaning tablet |
| Urgency | "You're slow cooking them in mold" (consequence urgency) |

## Iteration 1 (grandpa kept; status, problem aware)
- "If you've never cleaned your washer, this will be uncomfortable to hear. My grandpa is a retired
  washing machine repairman, and he always told me that washing machines are actually a filthy mess.
  I've got to say, I didn't believe him. You run detergent through it every time, how dirty could it
  get, right? But then the laundry started to smell. And not just a little musty. I mean, my friends at
  work were asking, 'Do your clothes always smell like this?' I was beyond embarrassed."
- Changed: avatar (broad: anyone who doesn't clean their machine), desire (status), awareness (problem
  aware, agitates). Urgency became "people are realizing how nasty their machines are, so it's selling
  out".
- Inherited: the grandpa authority. Same angle, mechanism, emotion and positioning.
- Slide wording: Angle "Same Angle (avoid due to awareness level)", Avatar "New BROAD avatar", Desire "New
  STATUS focused desire", Authority "!!!!! Same Grandpa authority" (the inherited learning), Awareness
  "Moved to PROBLEM aware", Urgency in red: "People realizing how nasty their machines are, so it's
  selling out".
- On screen: teardown footage (the inner drum pulled out, brown gunk on the outer tub) for "a filthy mess",
  an embarrassed young woman with black clothes, and a woman at work for the co-worker line.

## Iteration 2 (mistakes list; unaware, high intensity) – Uproot's top ad
- "How your dirty washer is slow cooking your clothes in mold, especially if you have pets. Let's fix
  three of the most common mistakes people make with their washing machines. Mistake number one: using
  too much detergent. More soap doesn't equal more clean…"
- Changed: angle (keep the machine clean and mold-free), awareness (unaware), emotion (high
  intensity), authority (the list format creates an education bias).
- Inherited: the mold urgency, moved to the first line. Avatar and desire stay close to the original.
- Spencer would add the grandpa to it, and Uproot runs that version too.
- Copy tip: "mistakes" and "red flags" beat "best ways".
- Slide wording: Angle "Will keep your washing machine clean", Avatar "Similar pet avatar", Desire "Same
  health desire", Authority "Built through execution (but I would test adding)", Awareness "Moved to
  unaware", Emotion "Low valence, high intensity", Urgency "!!!!! Brought urgency to the front".
- On screen: a big cyan headline overlay "SLOW COOKING YOUR CLOTHES IN MOLD" over the drum, the detergent
  drawer for "let's fix three of the most common mistakes", and a "MISTAKE #1" label before each mistake.
